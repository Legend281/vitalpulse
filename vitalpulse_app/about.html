About VitalPulse

We exist to
save lives.

VitalPulse is Cameroon's first real-time blood donation coordination network — connecting verified hospitals with nearby donors in seconds. We believe no one should die waiting for blood.

visibility

Our Mission

To eliminate blood shortage deaths in Cameroon by building a technology-driven ecosystem that connects every hospital, donor, and emergency in real time.

explore

Our Vision

A Cameroon where every patient in need of blood receives it within minutes — not hours. A decentralized, community-powered network that never sleeps.

Our Story

Built for Cameroon,
by Cameroonians.

The journey from an urgent problem to a national solution.

2024 — The Problem

A critical gap exposed

Cameroon faces a chronic blood shortage. With only 7 donated units per 1,000 people (vs. 30+ in developed nations), hospitals routinely turn away patients. The existing system relied on phone trees and word-of-mouth. We knew technology could do better.

warning
Timeline visual placeholder
lightbulb
2025 — The Idea

An idea takes shape

A team of Cameroonian healthcare professionals and software engineers came together. The insight was simple: every hospital has blood requests, every city has eligible donors — the missing piece was real-time coordination.

2025 — The Build

Building the Pulse Engine

Over 8 months, we developed the platform from the ground up — a real-time matching engine, multi-channel notification system (SMS, WhatsApp, in-app), hospital inventory management, donor engagement tiers, and a full admin control center.

construction
rocket_launch
2026 — Live & Growing

A national network takes flight

Today, VitalPulse serves 128+ verified hospitals across 7 regions with 15,400+ registered donors. Our average match time is under 30 seconds. And we're just getting started.
