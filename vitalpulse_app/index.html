verified_user Clinical Quality Assurance

Standardized Medical Safety Workflow

A clinical progression ensuring safety, donor health, and screening across every transfusion.

01
Initiation

Hospital Clinical Requisition

Authorized medical staff submit emergency transfusion requisitions through authenticated hospital workstation terminals.

02
Matching

Intelligent Donor Matching

The system locates compatible voluntary donors within the hospital catchment area and dispatches rapid notifications.

03
Evaluation

Donor Health Screening

Donors undergo standardized pre-donation medical evaluation including hemoglobin, blood pressure, and vital checks.

04
Testing

Certified Laboratory Testing

Mandatory transfusion-transmissible pathogen screenings and ABO/Rh blood grouping are completed prior to unit clearance.

05
Preservation

Cold-Chain Inventory Control

Cleared blood units are cataloged with unique serial tracking and held in temperature-regulated storage units.

06
Transfusion

Bedside Verification & Issue

Clinical staff conduct final double-identity verification and patient cross-match prior to bedside administration.
